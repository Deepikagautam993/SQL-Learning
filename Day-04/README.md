# 2. GROUP BY

GROUP BY is used to divide records into groups and apply aggregate calculations on each group separately.

This concept is extremely important for:
- business reporting,
- KPI dashboards,
- category-wise analysis,
- and region-wise reporting.

---

## Concepts Learned

- Grouping records
- Category-wise analysis
- Region-wise reports
- Department-wise summaries
- Group-based aggregation

---

## Real-World Examples

Companies use GROUP BY for:
- sales by category,
- revenue by region,
- employee analysis by department,
- customer segmentation,
- and performance tracking.

---

# 3. HAVING Clause

HAVING is used to filter grouped results after aggregation.

This is one of the most important advanced SQL concepts for interviews.

---

## Concepts Learned

- HAVING execution flow
- Difference between WHERE and HAVING
- Filtering grouped data
- Aggregate filtering logic

---

## Important Understanding

| Clause | Purpose |
|---|---|
| WHERE | Filters rows before grouping |
| HAVING | Filters groups after grouping |

This difference is one of the most frequently asked SQL interview questions.
